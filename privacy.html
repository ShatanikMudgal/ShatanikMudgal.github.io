Stickathlon privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Short version: Stickathlon has no accounts and doesn't ask for your email, phone number or location. If you use online features, the name, country and best results you choose to show are stored on our leaderboard. Ads and purchases are handled by Google. You can delete your online data at any time from Settings.

Who we are

Stickathlon is a mobile game made by an independent developer. For any privacy question or request, contact stickathlon.support@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Your athlete's look, name, bib number and country, your personal bests, coins, unlocked items and settings are saved only on your device. They are not sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes them.

This data is stored and sent through Supabase, our database and real-time service provider, on servers in the European Union (Paris, France). Your name and country on the leaderboard are visible to all players. Please don't use your full real name as a player name.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, including ads you can choose to watch for coins. Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and information about the ads you see, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later. See how Google uses data in ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings, under Google, then Ads.

Purchases

In-app purchases such as removing ads, coin packs and the season pass are processed by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details; Google only tells the game what you bought so it can unlock it.

What we don't do

  • We don't ask for or collect your email, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone or location.
  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't use analytics or tracking tools of our own.

Children

Stickathlon is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has shared personal data through the game, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights and deleting your data

You can delete your online data at any time: open Settings in the game, then Delete my online data. This removes your name and results from the leaderboard and forgets your guest ID. Your progress on the phone is not affected.

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU GDPR or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act), you also have the right to ask what data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to object to how it's used. Email stickathlon.support@gmail.com and we'll reply within 30 days. If you're in the EU, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database rules allow each player to change or delete only their own leaderboard entry.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For important changes, we'll also tell players in the game.
